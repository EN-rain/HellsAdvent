# 1. Core Combat & Comprehensive Upgrade Mechanics

## 1.1 Where to Access Upgrade Mechanics In-Game
All character upgrades, skill customizations, and progression systems are directly accessible through in-game interfaces:

| Upgrade Mechanic | Access Method | UI Location | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Abilities & Dual Talent Trees** | Press **`[K]`** or click **`[K] SKILLS`** | Bottom action bar on HUD | Open the Dual Talent Trees (Class + Spec), invest talent points, toggle choice fork skill mutations, and configure 4-slot hotbar + Ultimate [R]. |
| **Equipment & Armor Upgrades** | Press **`[I]`** or click **`[I] GEAR`** | Bottom action bar on HUD | Open inventory to equip Head, Body, and Leg armor for defense, damage reduction, and mobility boosts. |
| **Quartermaster Shop** | Interact with **Ume** in Kettle Camp | Kettle Safe Zone (Center) | Purchase armor pieces, stat upgrades, and supplies (Gauze Rolls, Sugar Tins, Lye Wash, Stim Sticks) with Gold. |
| **Specialization Promotion** | Auto-triggers at **Level 15** | Modal overlay | Choose between 3 advanced combat specializations per character (15 total in the game). Can also be previewed via class change UI. |
| **Instant Testing & Tuning** | Press **`[O]`** (Dev Settings) | Keyboard hotkey | Free developer tuners allowing instant +Level, +SP (Skill Points), +XP, godmode, infinite stamina, and no-cooldown testing. |

## 1.2 The Dual Talent Tree System (World of Warcraft Dragonflight Model)

The talent interface is divided into two distinct, interconnected trees accessed via the **`[K]`** menu under the **`🌳 DUAL TALENT TREES`** tab:

```
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------+
|         CLASS TALENT TREE          |       SPECIALIZATION TALENT TREE   |
|            (LEFT SIDE)             |              (RIGHT SIDE)          |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------+
| • Universal Archetype Passives     | • Specialization-Specific Arts     |
| • Baseline Ability Enhancers       | • 2-Way Choice Fork Skill Mutations|
| • Core Stat Boosts (ATK, HP, DEF)  | • Archetype Trait Amplification    |
| • Mobility & Survivability Roots   | • Spec Capstone Ultimate Synergy   |
+------------------------------------+------------------------------------+
```

### Node Shapes & Visual Vocabulary
The talent tree utilizes standardized geometric nodes to clearly convey function:
- **Square Nodes (Active Abilities)**:
  - Grants new combat techniques or baseline abilities.
  - Once learned, these abilities appear in your **Ability Deck** and can be assigned to Hotbar Keys **`[1]`**, **`[2]`**, **`[3]`**, **`[4]`**, or **`[R]`**.
- **Circle Nodes (Passives & Stat Enhancers)**:
  - Multi-rank investment nodes (e.g., Rank 1/1, Rank 1/2, Rank 2/2).
  - Directly scale core player attributes (Attack Power, Maximum Health, Stamina, Defense, Movement Speed, Critical Strike Chance, and Cooldown Reduction).
- **Octagon Nodes (Choice Nodes / Mutually Exclusive Skill Mutations)**:
  - Represented with an octagon boundary and `< >` toggle arrows.
  - Allows you to choose between two mutually exclusive mutations for a specific active skill, altering its tactical behavior, damage type, cooldown, or crowd-control properties.
  - Switching variants is **completely free** once the node is learned.

### Talent Point Economics & Respec Rules
- **Point Gain**: Players receive **1 Talent Point per level** starting at Level 2 (49 points by Level 50), plus **+1 Bonus Talent Point** at each milestone level (**Lv 15, Lv 30, Lv 45**) for a **Maximum Budget of 52 Talent Points at Level 50** (and up to 60 with quest/achievement bonuses).
- **Point Investment**: Left-click any valid node connected to an active parent node to invest a point.
- **Instant Refund & Respec**: 
  - **Right-click** any learned node to immediately refund the point and reallocate it elsewhere, provided no downstream children exclusively depend on it.
  - The **`FREE RESPEC`** button instantly refunds all invested points across both trees at zero cost.

## 1.3 How the Skill Tree & Skill Rank Track Affect Skills & Combat Stats

### 1. Automatic Skill Rank Track (Ranks 1–4, 0 Talent Points)
Every baseline active skill automatically evolves through **4 Ranks** at character milestones (**Lv 1 / Lv 15 / Lv 30 / Lv 45**) at **zero Talent Point cost**:
- **Rank 1 (Lv 1)**: Base damage, base cooldown, base area/duration.
- **Rank 2 (Lv 15)**: ~+13–15% damage, -0.4s to -0.5s cooldown, larger area or longer duration.
- **Rank 3 (Lv 30)**: ~+27–30% damage, -0.8s to -1.0s cooldown, expanded radius/duration.
- **Rank 4 (Lv 45)**: ~+40–45% damage, -1.2s to -1.6s cooldown, maximum baseline radius/duration.
- **Cooldown Floor**: Active skill cooldowns have a hard minimum floor of **50% of their Rank 1 base cooldown**.

### 2. Direct Attribute Scaling (Circle Nodes)
Every point invested in stat passives feeds directly into the character's real-time stat calculation pipeline:
- **Attack Power (ATK)**: Increases base weapon and skill damage pipeline before multipliers.
- **Maximum Health (HP)**: Enlarges health pool and increases survivability against high-damage boss telegraphs.
- **Maximum Stamina & Recovery**: Increases the stamina pool used for sprint, dodge rolls, and skills, while boosting recovery per second (+3/s to +6/s).
- **Armor & Defense (DEF)**: Reduces flat and percentage damage taken from enemy melee and projectile impacts.
- **Movement Speed**: Boosts baseline px/s velocity, allowing more forgiving positioning and kite spacing.
- **Critical Strike Chance & Multiplier**: Increases the frequency of critical hits (base 8% scaling upward to 25%+) and critical damage (1.5x up to 2.0x).
- **Cooldown Reduction (CDR)**: Lowers the recast timer across all equipped active combat arts (down to the 50% Rank 1 cooldown floor).

### 3. Skill Choice Forks (Octagon Nodes)
When a choice node is activated, the game inspects the chosen variant via `player.getSkillFork(skillId)` and dynamically alters the skill execution:
- **Damage Conversion**: Converts standard physical hits into elemental burns, corrosive rime, or high-stagger impacts.
- **Shape & Area Alteration**: Widens cone sweeps, extends laser reach, adds backward blast steps, or spawns piercing secondary projectiles.
- **Resource Adjustments**: Reduces stamina costs, refunds stamina on hit, or adds cooldown resets upon enemy execution.
- **Status Application**: Injects status effects such as Stun, Bleed, Brittle, Magnetic Pull, Disarm, or Armor Shred.

## 1.4 The 4-Tier Ultimate Ability System & Charge Economy

Ultimates do not require manual talent point expenditure; they **automatically evolve** as your character reaches critical level thresholds, strictly guaranteeing `Tier 1 < Tier 2 < Tier 3 < Tier 4` in both damage and spectacle:

| Tier | Level Requirement | Upgrade Phase | Visual & Gameplay Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Base)** | Level 1 | Baseline Ultimate | Core ultimate defining the class archetype, baseline animations, and initial damage values (`1.00x` scale). |
| **Tier 2 (Enhanced)** | Level 15 | Enhanced Art | **+35% damage** (`1.35x`), **+15% radius** (`1.15x`), **+20% duration** (`1.20x`), **+35% particles**, **+25% screen shake**, and Tier 2 class flourishes. |
| **Tier 3 (Transformed)** | Level 30 | Complete Mutation | **Fundamentally changes the ultimate mechanic, choreography, and Sakuga impact frame** (`1,100%` to `2,200% ATK`). Always exceeds Tier 2 of the same class. |
| **Tier 4 (Mastery)** | Level 45 | Apex Overdrive | **+50% damage over Tier 3** (`1.50x`), **+25% radius** (`1.25x`), **+35% duration** (`1.35x` capped at `10.0s` / `360px`), **+80% particles**, and bespoke Tier 4 finishing flourishes (e.g., *Rain of Swords*, *Thermobaric Nuke*, *18-Drone Armada*). |

### Ultimate Charge Economy (`0` to `100` Charge)
- **Passive Combat Trickle**: `+1.2 / sec` while engaged in combat.
- **Basic Attack Hit**: `+1.6` on primary target (`+0.45` per extra enemy cleaved).
- **Active Skill Hit**: `+3.0` on primary target (`+0.85` per extra enemy hit, hard-capped at **`+9.0` max charge per skill cast**).
- **Kill Bonuses**: `+4.0` per normal enemy, `+10.0` per Elite, `+25.0` per Boss.
- **Universal Charge Lock**: Ultimate charge gain is **paused (`pauseChargeWhileActive: true`) during any active ultimate cast or continuous ultimate window**.

## 1.5 Auto-Evolving Traits

Every character possesses an **Intrinsic Trait** (character-wide) and a **Class Trait** (specialization-specific). These traits scale automatically at three milestone ranks:
- **Rank I**: Level 1 (Baseline passive effect)
- **Rank II**: Level 15 (+50% to +75% increased potency)
- **Rank III**: Level 30 (+100% to +150% maximum effectiveness with additional secondary trigger)

## 1.6 Equipment & Armor Upgrades

Accessed via **`[I] GEAR`** or Kettle Camp Quartermaster **Ume**:
- **Head Armor**: Grants flat Defense, status resistance, and cooldown bonuses (e.g., Welding Goggles, Tempered Mask).
- **Body Armor**: Grants large Max HP increments and flat percentage Damage Reduction (e.g., Patched Vest, Slag Plate).
- **Leg Armor**: Grants Movement Speed and Dodge Roll stamina efficiency (e.g., Running Shoes, Greaves).
